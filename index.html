What connects these projects is a preference for explicit assumptions, useful comparisons, and conclusions that stay within the evidence.这些项目共同关注明确的假设、有意义的对照，以及与证据相称的结论。

01

Keep the comparison fair让对照公平

Freeze the vocabulary when comparing tokenizers. Reuse validation folds when comparing models. Change one interpretable part of the experiment at a time.比较分词策略时固定词表，比较模型时共用验证划分，让实验的变化具有可解释性。

02

Look beyond a single number不只看一个数字

Token counts sit beside timing. AUC sits beside precision–recall and calibration. Simulation estimates sit beside theory and uncertainty.token 数与耗时一起看，AUC 与精确率、召回率和校准一起看，模拟估计与理论及不确定性一起看。

03

Keep the boundaries visible说明工作的边界

Separate course components, team contributions, and AI assistance. Distinguish an archived result from a fresh run, and a learning exercise from a production system.区分课程复用、团队贡献和 AI 辅助；分清已有结果与新运行，也分清学习实验与生产系统。
